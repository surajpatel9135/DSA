1. Reverse traversal

You don't change the array. You simply visit its elements from right to left.

int[] arr = {10, 20, 30, 40, 50};

for (int i = arr.length - 1; i >= 0; i--) {
    System.out.print(arr[i] + " ");
}

Output:

50 40 30 20 10

But the array is still:

10 20 30 40 50

So:

Reverse traversal = read/visit the array backwards.
